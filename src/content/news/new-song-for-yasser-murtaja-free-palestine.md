Introducing *For Yasser Murtaja (Free Palestine)*, a poignant protest song that echoes the resilience and spirit of a young Gazan journalist whose life was cut short amidst the turmoil of the Great March of Return in 2018.

Yasser Murtaja's story is not just a narrative of tragedy; it's a testament to the unyielding spirit of those who yearn for freedom and justice in the face of oppression. As the first journalist to lose his life during the [Great March of Return](https://www.unrwa.org/campaign/gaza-great-march-return), his aspirations to explore the world beyond Gaza were tragically thwarted, yet his legacy continues to resonate with individuals worldwide.

In this track, I pay homage to Murtaja's courage and determination. I tried to encapsulate the essence of his journey - a journey marked by dreams deferred, but never forgotten. Let his life serve as a poignant reminder of the injustices endured by the people of Palestine and a call to action for global solidarity.

*For Yasser Murtaja (Free Palestine)* is more than just a song; it's a powerful anthem that amplifies the voices of those silenced by conflict and oppression. It stands as a tribute to Murtaja's memory and a rallying cry for a world where freedom knows no bounds. With this song I try to honor his legacy and advocate for a brighter future for Palestine.
